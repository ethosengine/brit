# Brit's delivery covenant

Habits are declared at the boundary whose behavior they describe. This order is
operator-approved priority, not a score inferred from semantic search. Work follows
prerequisites, then authored work order; unavailable work remains visible. At most
two habits may be active. This standalone scope does not grant extra attention when
embedded in Elohim: admission must respect the operator's shared two-habit budget.
All initial declarations are inactive; feature discovery never claims a commitment.

Green requires current, revision-bound evidence and independent acceptance where
specified. A successful test of a qualifier is not qualification of the product.
The compatibility horizon does not outrank safe, useful native work.
